## Description
At 3 a.m., the manic surge of bipolar disorder fractures the boundary between perception and reality. Through a collage of found footage and stop-motion animation, the film inhabits the restless rhythm of an unrelenting mind, where thoughts accelerate, memories collide, and ordinary objects take on a life of their own. Oscillating between exhilaration and unease, it offers an intimate glimpse into the disorienting intensity of a sleepless night.
